WallRoll

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026 · Last updated: September 18, 2026

Local project data, limited anonymous usage data. Everything you enter — wall measurements, project names, roll settings, and results — stays on your device and is never sent to us. WallRoll sends limited anonymous usage events to a server operated by the developer. There is no account, advertising, or third-party tracking.

Who we are

WallRoll (“the app”, “we”) is provided by Softgrove, an independent developer based in Japan. Contact: softgrove@duck.com.

What data the app handles

You may enter project names, wall measurements, door and window counts, roll dimensions, pattern repeat, waste settings, and prices (“your data”). Your data is stored only locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage framework. We never receive this data, and the app does not provide account sync or a cloud backup service.

No project content is sent. This includes project names, wall measurements, roll settings, and calculation results.

Anonymous usage analytics

WallRoll sends events for app launches, running a wallpaper calculation, viewing the first result, reaching an export or save limit as a non-Pro user (second project, PDF export, CSV export, share), Pro offer views, purchase-related actions (purchase start, completion, and failure with a fixed reason), and restoring a purchase. Each event contains the app version, a timestamp, the event name, and, when applicable, a paywall trigger or product SKU.

These events do not include any identifier: there is no UUID, account ID, advertising identifier (IDFA), or device identifier (IDFV) generated, stored, or sent. WallRoll does not access IDFA or IDFV. The events cannot be linked back to a specific device or person; the developer can only see aggregate counts. Events are not sent when running in the simulator or a debug build.

We use these events only to understand which features are used and where people stop in the app flow so we can improve WallRoll. We do not use them for advertising or tracking.

Each request contains the app identifier (a), app version (v), event name (e), timestamp (t), and a small properties object (p). Current properties contain only fixed internal screen or flow names and, where relevant, an App Store product identifier. They cannot contain user-entered content or free-form text. No device or installation identifier is included.

Cloudflare, Inc. hosts the endpoint as our infrastructure processor. Our Worker does not store source IP addresses, User-Agent headers, raw event bodies, or raw timestamps. It stores daily aggregate counts only for this app. Cloudflare may process network metadata to deliver and secure its service under its data-processing terms.

Export, sharing, and deletion

  • Export / Share: You can export a PDF summary or a CSV file of your project and share it using the system share sheet. Exported files are under your control once you share them; nothing is sent to us. Non-Pro accounts are limited to one saved project and cannot export; reaching that limit is what triggers the export-gate event described above.
  • Delete: You can delete any project at any time. Deleting the app also removes its on-device data.

Purchases and payments

WallRoll offers an optional Pro unlock as a one-time purchase, sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. Your purchase entitlement is verified on-device through Apple’s StoreKit framework.

Network connections

The measurement and calculation features work fully offline. When a network is available, WallRoll may send the anonymous usage events described above to a server operated by the developer. Apple’s system frameworks also communicate with the App Store for purchases and purchase restoration.

Disclaimer

Roll counts produced by WallRoll are estimates based on standard trim allowances and the measurements you enter. Your actual material needs depend on your wall irregularities, the specific paper you buy, and how it is hung. WallRoll does not provide wallpapering instruction or installation advice; follow your paper manufacturer's instructions and consult a qualified professional where needed.

Children

WallRoll is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region). The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third parties

WallRoll has no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. We use Cloudflare, Inc. only to host the anonymous event endpoint and aggregated database described above. We do not sell these events, provide them to third parties for their own purposes, use them for advertising, or use them to track you across apps or websites.
